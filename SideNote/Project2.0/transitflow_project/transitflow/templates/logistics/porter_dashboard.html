Perishable Cold-Chain Checklist

  • Check infrared core temperature upon receiving fresh produce/dairy/meat.
  • Verify reefer trailer thermostat matches required range (typically 2.0°C to 6.0°C).
  • Do not leave perishable cartons on unchilled loading docks exceeding 15 minutes.

Non-Perishable Stacking Rules

  • Heavy machinery and crated equipment must remain secured to pallet skids.
  • Cartons marked FRAGILE must never be double-stacked under bulk cargo.
  • Verify security seals are intact and report any sheared bolts immediately.
