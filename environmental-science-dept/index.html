Chair of Environmental Science

Dr. Elena Vance, Ph.D.

Professor of Wetland Biogeochemistry & Carbon Dynamics • Senior Fellow of the National Ecological Society

"Our planetary challenges do not wait for gradual solutions. Here at Greenwood, we equip you with analytical precision, remote sensing instruments, and boots-on-the-ground ecological training so your ideas translate directly into environmental resilience."

As Department Head, I am honored to lead a vibrant collective of over 30 faculty members, 450 undergraduate majors, and 120 graduate researchers. Our interdisciplinary curriculum merges atmospheric chemistry, watershed hydrology, conservation genomics, and environmental policy.

Whether you are stepping into our specialized trace metal analysis lab or traversing our 800-acre forest field station, you will discover that our faculty know every student by name. I welcome prospective students, current scholars, and community partners to reach out and visit our headquarters.

Academic Offerings

Programs Offered

Rigorous scientific training paired with direct field research, modern GIS modeling, and environmental policy analysis.

Undergraduate • 4 Years 120 Credits

B.S. in Environmental Science

Concentration: Conservation & Field Ecology

Focuses on biodiversity assessment, ecosystem restoration, forest dynamics, and wildlife population health with mandatory field station residencies.

Core Course Highlights:
ENV 201: Soil Science ENV 310: Wetland Ecology ENV 450: Capstone
Undergraduate • 4 Years 120 Credits

B.S. in Climate & Water Systems

Concentration: Hydrology & Atmospheric Dynamics

Blends fluid dynamics, meteorological satellite data, groundwater modeling, and flood mitigation policies for climate resilience.

Core Course Highlights:
ENV 240: Hydrogeology ENV 365: GIS Mapping ENV 420: Climate Risk
Graduate • 2 Years 36 Credits

M.S. in Environmental Dynamics

Thesis & Professional Non-Thesis Options

Tailored for aspiring environmental scientists, consulting analysts, and governmental agency specialists with advanced lab spectrometry.

Core Course Highlights:
ENV 601: Environmental Stats ENV 640: Biogeochemistry ENV 700: Thesis
Doctoral • 4-5 Years Fully Funded

Ph.D. in Global Earth Systems

Full Research Stipend & Tuition Remission

Original doctoral dissertation work tackling carbon sequestration, planetary boundary modeling, and extreme climate event prediction.
